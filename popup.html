DopaTube

Change YouTube playback speed with one click.

How to use

Click the numbered button between the YouTube logo and the search box.

Loading settings…

The number shows the playback speed. It stays the same when switching videos and resets to 1.0× when you reload the page.

In settings, choose 2–5 speeds in 0.05× increments. Clicking the “ド” logo can advance to the next speed, reset to 1.0×, or do nothing.

YouTube’s built-in speed controls also work. The button reflects the speed you select.

Open YouTube

No personal information or watch history is collected or sent to external servers.
